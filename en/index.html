About MK TECH

High-Technology
Service Maker

MK TECH Inc. designs and manufactures semiconductor wet and photo process equipment in-house, and carries out used-tool refurbishment, modification, relocation and process set-up as a single integrated service.

Integrated Production System

Next-Gen Co-Development

Field-Proven Engineering Team

Responsive Customer Care
