Key Features

Ultra-High Precision Coating

Industry-leading spin coating technology for advanced semiconductor lithography

High Throughput

Over 180 wafers per hour with optimized spin-dispense cycle and dual-arm wafer handling for maximum fab productivity.

Precision Dispense

Proprietary nozzle technology with multi-point dispense capability for optimal photoresist coverage and minimal edge bead.

Closed-Loop Control

Real-time spin speed monitoring with closed-loop feedback control ensuring <0.5% film thickness uniformity across the wafer.

Chemical Savings

Advanced dispense algorithms reduce photoresist consumption by up to 40% compared to conventional coating methods.

EBR System

Integrated Edge Bead Removal with solvent rinse and backside cleaning for defect-free lithography-ready wafers.

Multi-Layer Coating

Supports sequential multi-layer coating including BARC, photoresist, and topcoat in a single integrated process module.
