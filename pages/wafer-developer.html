Key Features

Precision Development Technology

Best-in-class wafer developing capabilities for advanced lithography nodes

High Throughput

Over 200 wafers per hour with multi-chamber parallel processing and optimized transfer robotics.

Puddle Development

Advanced puddle dispensing system with precise developer volume and timing control for exceptional CD uniformity.

Temperature Control

Precision temperature-controlled chambers maintaining ±0.05°C stability throughout the entire development process.

Modular Design

Configurable chamber layout supporting 4 to 16 process modules for scalable production capacity.

Defect Prevention

Integrated rinse and dry technology with N2 blow optimization eliminating watermark defects on developed patterns.

Automated recipe optimization with process parameter database and lot-to-lot tracking for consistent results.
